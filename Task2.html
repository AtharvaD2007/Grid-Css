1. What does fr stand for?

fr stands for fractional unit.It divides the available space in the grid container into equal parts and distributes it among columns or rows.

2. How many columns are created?

Three columns are created because grid-template-columns is set to 1fr 1fr 1fr, which defines three equal columns.

3. What happens when screen size is reduced?

When the screen size is reduced, the columns shrink proportionally to fit the available space.

4. Do items overflow or adjust?

The items adjust to the available space.

5. Why is fr better than px for layouts?

Unlike fixed pixel values, fr adjusts automatically for different screen sizes, making the design flexible and user-friendly.
